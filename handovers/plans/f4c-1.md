# f4c-1 -- poise and stagger, HP zones, co-op downed (spec 04 sections 2, 4, 5; D34)

Branch apx/f4c from origin/apx-main 801cf254. Protocol v2 (11-MASTER-PLAN section 6).

## Goal
- Poise: every creature and player has a poise pool by tier (fodder breaks in 1 hit, regular 2-3);
  hits drain it by weapon class; at 0 the target is STAGGERED 30 ticks (cannot act, takes 1.5x),
  then poise refills; regen 10 percent of max per second after 2 s without a hit.
- HP zones (D34): head 2.5, torso 1.0, limbs 0.6 damage multipliers. Zone pools: head 30, arms
  35, legs 35 percent of MAXHP (ASSUMED); torso is Barony's HP. A zone at 0 is DISABLED, never
  removed: legs -> no running (speed x0.5), arms -> no attacking. Head disabled: flag only.
  Hit location: player strikes by camera pitch (high head, middle torso or arms by side, low
  legs); creature strikes on players roll a zone weighted by attacker size.
- Downed (co-op): when a player's HP would reach 0 and another player is in the game, HP holds
  at 1 for 20 s (1000 ticks): crawl at 30 percent speed, no attacking, no HP loss; an ally within
  1.5 tiles for 3 s (150 ticks, reset by a hit on either) revives to 25 percent HP; timer end ->
  normal death (Barony ghost). Solo: one downed per floor. All values ASSUMED (spec 04).

## Tests
- Offline: tier table; fodder 1 hit, regular 2-3 hits with a sword; stagger ticks and 1.5x;
  regen; zone thresholds and disable; pitch -> zone; size-weighted roll frequencies over 10,000
  rolls; downed: lethal -> downed, timer -> dead, revive channel, interrupt, solo once per floor.
- Integration (one launch, APX_F4C_SELFTEST=1, -quickstart=barbarian -apxfloor=1): the self-test
  spawns 4 tiers next to the player and drives the REAL hook function with scripted hits; logs
  "[apx-f4c]" rows: hits to stagger per tier, staggered monster acts 0 times in 30 ticks, a
  legs-disabled player speed ratio, arms-disabled swing refused, downed with forced co-op
  (APX_DOWNED_FORCE_COOP=1): HP stays 1, revive restores 25 percent, timer kills.
- Expected: fodder 1, regular 2-3, stagger acts 0, speed ratio 0.5 (legs) and 0.3 (downed).
- Control that must fail: APX_POISE=0 APX_ZONES=0 APX_DOWNED=0 in the same harness -> the
  self-test's checks fail (no stagger, no zone disable, player dies).
